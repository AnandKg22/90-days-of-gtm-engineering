# Day 044: AI Research Agent

## Objective
Design and build an autonomous **AI Research Agent** that crawls company sites, extracts firmographic and technographic characteristics, tracks citation footprints for all findings, maps competitor layouts, and calculates data confidence metrics.

## Topics Covered
- Crawler Engine: Scrapes HTML elements, headers, and meta tags.
- Information Extraction: Isolating business summaries and technical stacks.
- Citation tracking: Mapping facts back to direct source tags.
- Competitive Positioning: Resolving rivals and market niches.
- Information Confidence Scoring models.

## Subtopics (Developed in Notes)
- HTML Parsing & BeautifulSoup scrapers
- Regex pattern matching for GTM indicators
- Fact Hallucination Audits & citation mapping
- Competitor mapping algorithms
- Confidence metrics weights matrix
