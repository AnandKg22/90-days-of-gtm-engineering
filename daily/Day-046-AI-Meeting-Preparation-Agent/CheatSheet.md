# Cheat Sheet - AI Meeting Preparation

This cheat sheet compiles pre-call qualification frameworks, SPIN prompts, and objection handling formats.

---

## 1. SPIN Selling Reference Questions

| SPIN Stage | Focus | Example GTM Prompt |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Situation** | Process audit | "How are you currently routing leads from your web form to sales reps?" |
| **Problem** | Friction points | "What is the typical delay or latency in that routing chain?" |
| **Implication** | Cost of failure | "When routing delays exceed 4 hours, what percentage of leads go cold?" |
| **Need-Payoff** | Value utility | "If leads reached reps in under 60 seconds, how would that impact win rates?" |

---

## 2. MEDDPICC Checklist

Before running a discovery call, ensure your notes cover the MEDDPICC parameters:
*   **M (Metrics)**: What is the quantified financial target? (e.g. Save $45k/yr).
*   **E (Economic Buyer)**: Who signs the contract? (Identify early in meeting).
*   **D (Decision Criteria)**: Security (SOC 2), API reliability, cost limits.
*   **D (Decision Process)**: What legal/technical approvals are required?
*   **P (Paper Process)**: Contract MSA review pipeline timeline.
*   **I (Identify Pain)**: Lead leakage or manual finance sync delays.
*   **C (Champion)**: The VP of Sales Operations driving adoption.
*   **C (Competition)**: In-house developers or alternative SaaS platforms.

## 3. Standard 30-Minute Sales Meeting Agenda
*   **00:00 - 00:05 | Intros & Framing**: Align on goals, check if timing is still good.
*   **00:05 - 00:15 | Discovery (SPIN)**: Rep asks diagnostic questions; prospect maps out internal pains.
*   **00:15 - 00:25 | Solution walkthrough**: Tailored walkthrough addressing the identified pain points.
*   **00:25 - 00:30 | Next steps & Timeline**: Align on clear next actions and follow-up syncs.
