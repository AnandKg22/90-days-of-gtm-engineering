# Project Assignment - Day 046: Meeting Brief Generator

This project requires developing a Python Meeting Brief Generator. It acts as a pre-call sales intelligence compiler, helping Account Executives prepare for upcoming meetings.

---

## 🎯 Requirements

Your Brief Generator must:
1.  **Ingest Meeting Meta Details**:
    *   Accept company size, industry, current technology stack, and competitors.
2.  **Analyze Stakeholder Profiles**:
    *   Detail stakeholder title, department, motivations, and pain points.
3.  **Compile Competitive Battle Cards**:
    *   Evaluate the prospect's technographic stack and explain our competitive advantages.
4.  **Formulate SPIN Discovery Questions**:
    *   Generate custom situation, problem, implication, and need-payoff questions tailored to the prospect's pain points.
5.  **Predict Objections**:
    *   Anticipate common objections based on the stakeholder's role (e.g. security concerns for CTOs, cost concerns for CEOs) and provide reframing responses.
6.  **Schedule Meeting Agendas**:
    *   Outline a structured 30-minute agenda allocating time for discovery, solution overview, and next steps.

---

## 💻 Deliverable Code

A complete, working Meeting Brief Generator script has been created and is available in [Code/meeting_brief_generator.py](Code/meeting_brief_generator.py). It parses mock meeting objects for Stark Industries and Wayne Enterprises, compiling briefing dossiers in the terminal.
